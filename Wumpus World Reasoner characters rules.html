Rules

The goal

Find the gold and climb out through the entrance at [1,1].

The cave

A 4 by 4 grid. The explorer starts at [1,1], which is always safe. It moves one square up, down, left or right. No diagonals.

The wumpus

One monster hides in the cave. Walk into its square and it eats you.

Pits

Bottomless holes. Step into one and you fall forever.

One arrow

It flies in a straight line until it hits the wumpus or a wall. A scream means the wumpus is dead.

The gold

The explorer grabs it when it sees a glitter in its square.

The explorer can only sense its own square
  • Stench means the wumpus is in a neighboring square.
  • Breeze means a pit is in a neighboring square.
  • Glitter means the gold is right here.
  • Scream means the arrow killed the wumpus.
How it decides
  • It checks every possible cave that fits what it has sensed so far.
  • A square is safe only if it is safe in all of those caves.
  • It never guesses. If no square is proven safe, it uses the arrow or goes home.
